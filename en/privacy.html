Privacy Policy

HealthDock for iPhone and Mac · Effective November 23, 2026

Help & Support中文

In short

Your health data only travels between your own iPhone and your own Mac. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, receive or store any of your data, and cannot see it.

What we read

With your permission, the iPhone app reads the Health categories you allow, such as steps, distance, active and resting energy, exercise and stand time, heart rate and related measures (resting heart rate, heart rate variability and more), ECG, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, sleep, body weight and measurements, blood pressure, body temperature, blood glucose, workouts and workout routes, and mindfulness. You can change this anytime in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → HealthDock.

HealthDock only reads. It never writes to or changes Health data. The developer never uses your health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and never shares it with third parties.

Where the data goes

  • Data goes directly from your iPhone to the Mac you paired it with, over Bluetooth or your local network. It never crosses the internet or any server.
  • Transfers are encrypted with keys created by the two devices during pairing. Only your paired Mac can read them.
  • On your Mac, data is kept in the app's own storage and in the Health Archive folder you choose, under your control.

Data you choose to give to AI

HealthDock itself does not connect to or call any AI service. Each of the following happens only when you act, and you hand the data to an AI tool of your own choosing:

  • Copy for AI: when you click it, your health summary is copied to your Mac's clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you.
  • AI guide files in your Health Archive (such as CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md): reference notes for AI tools you run on your own computer when they read your archive. HealthDock never launches those tools.
  • Local AI connection (Mac feature, off by default): once you turn it on, only AI tools on this Mac that you configure can run read-only queries. It listens on this Mac only and accepts no connections from your local network or the internet. You can turn it off anytime. If you also turn on “Allow AI to log events and goals”, those AI tools can write the life events and goals you tell them into events/ and goals.json in your Health Archive. They cannot change any health data and never write to the Health app.

Once you give data to an AI tool or service, that service's own privacy policy applies. If the tool sends content to a cloud model, the data leaves your device. Only use services you trust.

Why we ask for permissions

On the iPhone:

  • Health: to read the data you allow and sync it to your Mac.
  • Bluetooth: to talk to your paired Mac.
  • Local Network: to connect directly to your Mac on the same Wi‑Fi and move large amounts of history quickly.
  • Camera: only to scan the pairing code on your Mac. Images are not saved or uploaded.
  • Notifications (optional): a local reminder after 3 days without a sync.

On the Mac:

  • Bluetooth: to talk to your paired iPhone.
  • Local Network: to receive large amounts of history from your iPhone on the same Wi‑Fi; when the local AI connection is on, to answer read-only queries on this Mac only (127.0.0.1).
  • Folders you choose: HealthDock only reads and writes the Health Archive folder you pick and the folder you choose for auto-importing encrypted packs.

Purchases

Subscriptions, the one-time purchase and the trial use Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; the developer never receives your payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks Apple-signed receipts on your device.

Diagnostic log

The app keeps a local log of events such as connections and transfer results. It contains no health values and stays on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to send it with an email from Settings → Feedback.

What's not included

The apps have no accounts, no ads, no analytics or third-party SDKs, and no tracking.

This website

The Apple Health converters on gethealthdock.com run entirely in your browser; the export files you select are never uploaded to any server. This website uses no analytics or tracking scripts.

If you join the launch waitlist, your email is sent to our support inbox through the third-party form service FormSubmit. It is used only to tell you when the app launches, never for anything else, and is not shared with any other third party. To have it deleted, email the address below.

Deleting your data

Unpair and delete HealthDock on your iPhone to remove its sync records there. Delete HealthDock and the Health Archive folder on your Mac to remove all data on the Mac. Your original data in the Health app is not affected.

Children

HealthDock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@gethealthdock.com.
