Privacy Policy

HealthDock for iPhone and Mac · Effective November 17, 2026

Help & Support中文

In short

Your health data only travels between your own iPhone and your own Mac. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, receive or store any of your data, and cannot see it.

What we read

With your permission, the iPhone app reads the Health categories you allow, such as steps, distance, active and resting energy, exercise and stand time, heart rate and related measures (resting heart rate, heart rate variability and more), ECG, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, sleep, body weight and measurements, blood pressure, body temperature, blood glucose, workouts and workout routes, and mindfulness. You can change this anytime in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → HealthDock.

HealthDock only reads. It never writes to or changes Health data. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never shared with third parties.

Where the data goes

  • Data goes directly from your iPhone to the Mac you paired it with, over Bluetooth or your local network. It never crosses the internet or any server.
  • Transfers are encrypted with keys created by the two devices during pairing. Only your paired Mac can read them.
  • On your Mac, data is kept in the app's own storage and in the Health Archive folder you choose, under your control.

Why we ask for permissions

  • Health: to read the data you allow and sync it to your Mac.
  • Bluetooth: to talk to your paired Mac.
  • Local Network: to connect directly to your Mac on the same Wi‑Fi and move large amounts of history quickly.
  • Camera: only to scan the pairing code on your Mac. Images are not saved or uploaded.
  • Notifications (optional): a local reminder after 3 days without a sync.

Purchases

Subscriptions, the one-time purchase and the trial use Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; the developer never receives your payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks Apple-signed receipts on your device.

Diagnostic log

The app keeps a local log of events such as connections and transfer results. It contains no health values and stays on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to send it with an email from Settings → Feedback.

What's not included

No accounts, no ads, no analytics or third-party SDKs, no tracking.

Deleting your data

Unpair and delete HealthDock on your iPhone to remove its sync records there. Delete HealthDock and the Health Archive folder on your Mac to remove all data on the Mac. Your original data in the Health app is not affected.

Children

HealthDock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@gethealthdock.com.
