Life Events & AI Health Journal FAQ

Does logging a life event or goal write anything back into the iPhone Apple Health app?

Never. HealthDock on iPhone is strictly read-only for Apple Health. Coming in HealthDock 1.3, life events and goals are stored only in plain local files (events/YYYY-MM.jsonl and goals.json) inside your chosen Mac Health Archive folder and are never written back to Apple Health.

Can my AI log or delete events without my permission?

No. All four MCP write tools in HealthDock 1.3—log_event, delete_event, set_goal, and deactivate_goal—are turned off by default. Your AI client can only record or update life events and goals after you explicitly enable write access in HealthDock Settings.

Does HealthDock claim that alcohol, travel, or other logged events changed my HRV or sleep?

No. Everyday health metrics shift with many overlapping factors at once. HealthDock surfaces your logged life events alongside sleep, resting heart rate, and HRV strictly so you can compare dates side by side—never implying cause and effect and never providing medical advice.

How are bedtime and wake-time goals evaluated if I take a short evening nap?

In HealthDock 1.3, bedtime and wake-time goals (such as falling asleep before 23:30) are evaluated using the longest sleep segment of that night. Brief early-evening naps are ignored so they do not count toward or distort your bedtime streak.

Who can see the life events and goals I tell my AI?

HealthDock itself has no servers, no accounts, and uploads nothing, and its MCP server runs only on your Mac. However, whatever you say to your AI client—and any archive data your AI queries—is sent to the AI service provider you selected under that provider’s privacy policy. Read our Privacy Policy.
