Apple Health on Mac FAQ

Why isn’t there an official Apple Health app on macOS?

Apple keeps HealthKit natively on iPhone, Apple Watch, and iPadOS. While iCloud syncs Health data between your iPhone and iPad, Apple does not expose a native Health app or local folder export on macOS. You can export a full ZIP manually via Apple’s iPhone Health export guide, or sync automatically with a local companion app.

Does HealthDock require an account or send health data to any server?

No. HealthDock has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no cloud relay. Data travels directly between your paired iPhone and Mac using local encryption keys created during QR pairing.

Does HealthDock modify or write to Apple Health?

Never. HealthDock requests read-only access to the Health categories you choose and never writes, alters, or deletes anything inside Apple Health.

What macOS and iOS versions are required?

HealthDock for Mac requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later; HealthDock for iPhone requires iOS 17 or later. Both devices need Bluetooth enabled for daily background sync (and the same Wi-Fi network for fast multi-year history catch-up).
