What Belongs in a Helpful Morning Apple Watch Summary?

Most people searching for an AI daily health report don’t want a mysterious 0–100 “readiness” score invented by a black-box formula, nor do they want generic textbook averages that ignore individual physiology. What actually helps over morning coffee is a concise, single-page check-in anchored to your own recent range: how long you slept, how your resting heart rate and HRV sit relative to your last 30 days, what training you completed yesterday, and any travel or schedule notes you logged nearby.

Letting Your Own AI Write journal/YYYY-MM-DD-daily.md

If you already use Claude Code, Codex, or another local agent on your Mac—querying either the plain archive files described in Apple Health on Mac or the local tools in our Apple Health MCP Server guide—you can have your AI save its morning summary to journal/YYYY-MM-DD-daily.md with YAML front matter containing type: daily-report, generator: (your AI tool’s name), and date.

When HealthDock 1.3 detects that file for today, it skips generating the built-in local report and opens your AI’s file directly from “Today’s Report” in the menu bar. Note that HealthDock never runs or schedules your AI tool on a timer—any daily automation stays completely under your own AI tool’s setup.

Daily Health Report FAQ

Does HealthDock’s built-in daily health report send my Apple Watch data to an AI model?

No. Coming in HealthDock 1.3, the built-in daily report is generated locally on your Mac using deterministic rules after your morning sync—it uses zero AI and contacts zero external servers, saving a plain Markdown file to journal/healthdock-daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md.

How can I replace the built-in report with a report written by my own AI?

In HealthDock 1.3, if your own AI workflow (such as Claude Code or Codex) writes a Markdown file to journal/YYYY-MM-DD-daily.md with YAML front matter (type: daily-report, generator: your AI tool’s name, and date), HealthDock skips generating its built-in local report for that date and opens your AI-written report directly from the Mac menu bar.

Does HealthDock run my AI tool on a morning timer?

No. HealthDock never launches, invokes, or schedules third-party AI tools. If you want an AI-authored morning report waiting for you each day, scheduling is handled entirely by your own AI tool or local automation.

Does the morning report use clinical cutoffs or provide medical diagnosis?

Never. HealthDock’s built-in report compares last night’s sleep, resting heart rate, and HRV solely against your own 30-day personal mean ± standard deviation. It uses zero medical thresholds, states plain facts with gentle everyday observations, and is not a medical diagnosis.

Are my sleep or heart rate numbers shown in macOS notification banners?

No. When your daily health report is ready on your Mac in HealthDock 1.3, the optional notification simply lets you know the report is available and never includes any health values in the notification text. See our Privacy Policy.
