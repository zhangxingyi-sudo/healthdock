Get started

  1. Install the free HealthDock for Mac.
    Download on the Mac App Store
  2. Open HealthDock on your iPhone and allow access to Health data. We recommend “Turn On All” and choosing “All” for history.
  3. On your Mac, click the HealthDock icon in the menu bar, choose “Show pairing QR code”, and scan the code with your iPhone.
  4. Your 7-day free trial starts after pairing and syncs your last 14 days. After that, HealthDock syncs in the background every day. You don't need to open it.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later and a Mac with macOS 14 or later, with Bluetooth on. To move years of history, keep both on the same Wi‑Fi.

FAQ

Why didn't it sync automatically?

  • Don't swipe HealthDock away in the app switcher. iOS won't wake it in the background after that. Opening it once fixes it.
  • Keep Bluetooth on on both devices, keep HealthDock running on your Mac, and keep your iPhone nearby.
  • Turn on “Sync Reminder” in Settings to get a nudge after 3 days without a sync.

Full history is slow or stuck

Full history uses a direct local network connection to your Mac. Put both devices on the same Wi‑Fi and turn on Local Network in Settings → HealthDock. Keep HealthDock open while it transfers.

Only the last 30 days synced

You chose 30 days of history when granting access. Go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → HealthDock and change history to “All”. HealthDock reads the older data next time you open it.

Pricing and trial

The Mac app is free. The iPhone app includes a 7-day free trial that syncs your last 14 days. Full history and ongoing sync are available as a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase, priced as shown in the App Store. The trial never charges you. After switching phones or reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchases”.

Where is my data on the Mac?

Choose “Open Data Folder” in the Mac menu. You'll find plain files organized by day and month, including workouts and routes. context.md is a ready-made health summary (recent vs. baseline, unusual days, long-term trends) you can give to the AI you use.

How do I delete my data?

Unpair and delete HealthDock on your iPhone. On your Mac, delete HealthDock and its data folder. HealthDock never changes anything in the Health app.

When automatic sync isn't available

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Export to Mac and AirDrop the encrypted pack. Your Mac imports it automatically.
