Get Started in 2 Minutes

  1. Install the free HealthDock for Mac from the Mac App Store.
    Download HealthDock for Mac (Free)
  2. Install HealthDock on your iPhone and allow Health access (we recommend tapping Turn On All and choosing All for history).
  3. Scan the QR code: click the HealthDock icon in your Mac menu bar, choose Show Pairing QR Code, and scan with your iPhone.
  4. Done: your 7-day free trial syncs the last 14 days in under a minute, then keeps your Mac updated automatically in the background every day.

Requires iOS 17+ and macOS 14+ with Bluetooth on. For syncing years of history, keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is HealthDock different from Claude’s native Apple Health integration?

Claude’s built-in Apple Health connector is currently limited to US accounts, requires a paid Claude plan, works only inside the iOS app, and sends data to the cloud. HealthDock works in every country, keeps 100% of your data in plain files on your own Mac, and works with Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, ChatGPT, and Obsidian.

Where does my health data go? Do you run any servers?

No servers, no accounts, no analytics. Data is encrypted end-to-end with keys generated on your iPhone and Mac during QR pairing and moves only over Bluetooth or your local Wi-Fi network.

What files are saved on my Mac?

Inside the Health Archive folder you choose (default ~/Documents/HealthDock), you get context.md (AI-ready baseline & anomaly summary), daily/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD.json, archive/YYYY.md, raw routes/ and ecg/ JSON, plus optional CLAUDE.md and 6 health-analysis skills.

How do pricing and the 7-day free trial work?

HealthDock for Mac is free. The iPhone app includes a 7-day free trial that syncs your last 14 days—with zero automatic billing when the trial ends. Unlock full history and ongoing sync with a yearly plan or a one-time lifetime purchase via Apple In-App Purchase. Data already on your Mac always stays yours to view and export.

Why didn’t automatic sync run in the background?
  • Don’t swipe HealthDock closed in the iOS app switcher—iOS won’t wake force-quit apps in the background. Opening it once resumes background sync.
  • Keep Bluetooth on for both devices, keep HealthDock running in your Mac menu bar, and bring your iPhone near your Mac.
  • Enable Sync Reminder in iPhone Settings to get a local nudge if 3 days pass without a sync.
Only the last 30 days synced—how do I sync my full history?

When granting Health permissions, 30 days was selected instead of All. Open iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → HealthDock and set History to All. Return to HealthDock and it will automatically read your earlier years.

How do I delete my data?

Unpair and delete HealthDock on your iPhone, and delete HealthDock and your Health Archive folder on your Mac. HealthDock is strictly read-only and never modifies Apple Health.
