Why Use HealthDock’s Local Converter?

      

        

          
AI-Ready Signal, Not Raw XML

          

            Raw Apple Health exports are 300MB–2GB—far too large for Claude or ChatGPT. HealthDock computes 7d/30d/90d rolling baselines, flags ≥2 SD unusual days, and extracts yearly trends & 8-week training volume into ~2KB of Markdown.
          

        

        

          
Smart Cleaning Built In

          

            Merges overlapping sleep segments (18:00–12:00 night windows), avoids Watch + iPhone step double-counting, normalizes lb/kJ/SpO2 units, and filters corrupted third-party timestamps automatically.
          

        

        

          
Works Globally & Offline

          

            Anthropic’s native iOS Health connector is US-only and mobile-only. HealthDock works in every country and outputs plain Markdown & CSV for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, ChatGPT, and Excel.

  1. Open the Apple Health app on your iPhone and tap your profile icon in the top right.
    2. 
        
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Export All Health Data → Export.

  4. Drop export.zip directly into the converter box at the top of this page—no unzipping needed.

Frequently Asked Questions


      

        Does my Apple Health export.xml ever leave my computer?
        
No. Parsing runs 100% locally in a browser Web Worker using 4MB file slices. There is no server, no API endpoint, no user account, and no tracking script.

      


      

        Which Apple Health metrics and workouts are supported?
        
All 15 key daily indicators—Resting Heart Rate, HRV (SDNN), Walking Heart Rate, Sleep (total, Deep, REM), Steps, Active Energy, Exercise Minutes, Blood Oxygen (SpO₂), Respiratory Rate, VO₂max, Systolic & Diastolic Blood Pressure, and Weight—plus 35 official Apple Health workout types with duration, active kcal, average heart rate, and distance.

      


      

        Will it crash on a 500MB or 1GB Apple Health export?
        
No. Unlike naive web converters that call DOMParser on the entire file, HealthDock streams 4MB chunks sequentially (and streams DEFLATE-compressed export.zip via the browser’s native DecompressionStream), keeping RAM usage low.

      


      

        Is this medical advice or a diagnostic tool?
        
No. HealthDock only summarizes and formats your own Apple Health records so you and your chosen AI or spreadsheet tools can review trends. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.
