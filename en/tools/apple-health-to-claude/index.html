Privacy & Technical FAQ

      

        Is my health data really never uploaded?
        
Yes. This is a pure static GitHub Pages site with zero backend APIs, zero analytics, and zero cookies. All parsing happens in your own browser’s Web Worker. You can open DevTools → Network (or turn off Wi-Fi after the page loads) to verify zero bytes leave your machine.

      

      

        Why is this better than pasting raw XML or CSV into Claude?
        
A typical Apple Health export contains millions of raw rows (300MB–2GB), which exceeds every LLM context window. HealthDock’s context.md condenses years of records into ~2KB of high-signal Markdown: 7d/30d/90d rolling baselines, statistically unusual days (≥2 SD), yearly trends, and 8-week training load.

      

      

        Does it work if I’m outside the US or using Claude Desktop / Claude Code?
        
Yes. Unlike Anthropic’s US-only mobile Apple Health connector, context.md is plain Markdown—paste it into Claude web, Claude Desktop, ChatGPT, or Gemini anywhere in the world, or let HealthDock for Mac maintain a full local workspace for Claude Code and Codex.

      

      

        Is this medical advice?
        
No. This tool only summarizes and organizes your personal Apple Health records. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions or decisions.
