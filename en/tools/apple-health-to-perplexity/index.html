Perplexity & Privacy FAQ

      

        Why does Perplexity reject Apple Health export.zip directly?
        
Perplexity Spaces and chat uploads cap individual files far below a typical 300MB–2GB Apple Health export. HealthDock locally aggregates millions of raw samples into ~2KB of clean Markdown that fits effortlessly in any prompt or Space.

      

      

        Can I combine context.md with Academic source filtering?
        
Yes—that is Perplexity’s strongest advantage. Turn on Academic sources in the Perplexity search bar so every threshold or recommendation links directly to peer-reviewed papers.

      

      

        Is this medical advice?
        
No. HealthDock formats your Apple Health metrics for self-tracking and literature research only. Always consult a qualified physician for medical diagnosis or treatment.
