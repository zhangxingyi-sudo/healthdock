FAQ

How are overlapping Apple Watch and iPhone step counts handled?

When both your Apple Watch and iPhone record cumulative metrics (steps, active energy, exercise minutes) on the same day, the converter aggregates by device source and prioritizes your Apple Watch total to prevent 2× double-counting. Sleep intervals are also merged across overlapping segments using an 18:00–12:00 night window.

What columns are included in the Daily Summary CSV?

Every row is one calendar date (YYYY-MM-DD) with your selected metrics (resting_hr_avg, hrv_sdnn_ms, sleep_min, deep_min, rem_min, steps, active_kcal, exercise_min, spo2_pct, resp_rate, vo2max, bp_sys, bp_dia, weight_kg) plus workout_count and workout_min.

Is any of my health data sent over the internet?

Never. All streaming and CSV/JSON generation happens strictly inside your local browser tab via a Web Worker.
