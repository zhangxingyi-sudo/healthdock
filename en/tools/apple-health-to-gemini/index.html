Gemini & Privacy FAQ

      

        Why does raw export.xml still fail in Gemini’s 1M token window?
        
A multi-year export.xml is often 500MB–2GB of verbose XML attributes—more than 100 million tokens. HealthDock strips duplicates and verbose metadata locally, producing a ~2KB baseline summary and a clean daily JSON (typically 100–400KB for multiple years) that Gemini ingests effortlessly.

      

      

        How do I turn off Gemini Apps Activity before pasting health data?
        
Visit your Google Gemini Apps Activity settings and pause activity tracking if you prefer not to have chats saved or reviewed. HealthDock itself is a static browser-only tool with zero backend servers.

      

      

        Does NotebookLM accept Markdown or JSON files?
        
NotebookLM accepts plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md) sources directly. Use our one-click Download .txt (NotebookLM) button for immediate upload compatibility.

      

      

        Is this medical advice?
        
No. HealthDock formats your personal Apple Health metrics for self-tracking and discussion. Consult a qualified doctor for medical interpretation or treatment decisions.
