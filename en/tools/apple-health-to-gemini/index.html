Gemini & Privacy FAQ

      

        What are Google Gemini’s actual context window limits by plan?
        
Per Google’s official Gemini Apps limits page, context capacity is 32k tokens without an AI plan, 128k tokens on AI Plus, and 1 million tokens on AI Pro & AI Ultra (subject to updates; as of Nov 2026). Raw 500MB XML exceeds even 1M tokens—whereas HealthDock’s ~2KB context.md (~600 tokens) fits every tier, and the clean Multi-Year JSON fits comfortably on AI Pro/Ultra.

      

      

        How do I manage Gemini Apps history before pasting health data?
        
Follow Google’s official Gemini Apps history guide to turn off Keep Activity (formerly Gemini Apps Activity) if you prefer not to have chats saved to your Google Account or used to improve models. HealthDock itself is a static browser-only tool with zero backend servers.

      

      

        What file formats and limits does NotebookLM (Gemini Notebook) support?
        
Per Google’s official NotebookLM / Gemini Notebook source docs, supported sources include .txt, .md, .csv, and .pdf (up to 500,000 words or 200 MB per uploaded file, as of Nov 2026). Use our one-click Download .txt (NotebookLM) button for immediate upload compatibility.

      

      

        Is this medical advice?
        
No. HealthDock formats your personal Apple Health metrics for self-tracking and discussion. Consult a qualified doctor for medical interpretation or treatment decisions.
