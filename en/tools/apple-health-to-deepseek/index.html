DeepSeek & Local LLM Privacy FAQ

      

        Why pair HealthDock with local Ollama instead of cloud DeepSeek?
        
Personal health data is among the most sensitive files you own. By converting export.zip locally in your browser and running DeepSeek-R1 locally via Ollama or LM Studio, zero bytes of your health history ever leave your Mac. If you do use DeepSeek’s web chat, check Settings → Data controls to opt out of model training and review DeepSeek’s official Privacy Policy.

      

      

        Will small 8B or 14B local models hallucinate on Apple Health data?
        
Small models struggle when forced to do arithmetic across thousands of raw CSV rows. HealthDock pre-computes exact 7d/30d/90d averages, ≥2 SD unusual days, yearly averages, and weekly training hours locally in JavaScript—so local reasoning models focus purely on interpreting verified numbers.

      

      

        Is this medical advice?
        
No. This tool summarizes your personal Apple Health records for private self-tracking. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical diagnosis or decisions.
