How HealthDock Cleans Apple Watch Sleep & HRV Data

18:00–12:00 Night Windows

Sleep that starts at 11:15 PM and ends at 6:45 AM belongs to one night—not split across two dates. Overlapping Watch/iPhone/app intervals are merged before summing minutes.

Anomalous Record Filtering

Third-party apps occasionally write zero-duration or future-dated sleep rows into Apple Health. HealthDock filters corrupted timestamps automatically so your baseline stays clean.

Personal Baseline First

HRV (SDNN) varies hugely from person to person (20 ms to 120+ ms can all be normal). Comparing your last 7 days to your own 90-day mean ± SD surfaces real recovery shifts.

Sleep & HRV Analyzer FAQ

Why is my Deep or REM sleep blank on older dates?

Apple introduced native WatchOS sleep stages (Core, Deep, REM) in watchOS 9 (late 2022). Older nights—or nights tracked without an Apple Watch—only record total asleep duration, which HealthDock still charts cleanly.

Does Apple Watch report HRV as SDNN or RMSSD?

Apple Health stores HRV natively as HKQuantityTypeIdentifierHeartRateVariabilitySDNN (SDNN in milliseconds). HealthDock preserves the exact Apple Health SDNN numbers without proprietary black-box “readiness” formulas.

Is this medical or sleep-disorder diagnosis?

No. Consumer wearables estimate sleep stages and HRV for personal wellness tracking only. Consult a qualified physician or sleep specialist for medical evaluation.
