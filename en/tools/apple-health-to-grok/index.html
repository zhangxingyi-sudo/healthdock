Grok & Privacy FAQ

      

        Why use HealthDock instead of uploading export.zip to Grok?
        
Raw Apple Health exports contain millions of sensor samples across 300MB–2GB of XML, which exceeds Grok’s upload ceiling and wastes context on raw boilerplate. HealthDock cleans duplicates locally (merging overlapping sleep windows and preventing Watch + iPhone step double-counting) and outputs a crisp ~2KB Markdown summary.

      

      

        Does my Apple Health file ever touch HealthDock’s servers?
        
Never. HealthDock has zero backend servers, zero accounts, and zero analytics. Parsing runs strictly inside your browser’s Web Worker—you can turn off Wi-Fi after loading the page and it still works.

      

      

        Is this medical advice?
        
No. This tool summarizes your personal Apple Health records for informational self-tracking only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice or diagnosis.
