Obsidian & Privacy FAQ

      

        Why are all YAML property values pure numbers instead of strings with units?
        
Obsidian Properties and Dataview math functions (`average()`, `sum()`, sorting, and comparison filters like `WHERE sleep_hours < 6.5`) require numeric YAML values rather than strings like `"6.5h"`. Units are encoded clearly in the key names (`sleep_hours`, `hrv_ms`, `resting_hr`, `weight_kg`).

      

      

        Does this fix Apple Watch + iPhone step double-counting and split sleep segments?
        
Yes. HealthDock’s local Web Worker merges overlapping sleep intervals across each 18:00–12:00 night window and deduplicates Apple Watch + iPhone cumulative step/calorie totals before generating your Obsidian Markdown.

      

      

        Is any data sent to a server?
        
Zero bytes. Parsing happens entirely offline inside your browser—matching Obsidian’s local-first philosophy.
