ChatGPT & Privacy FAQ

      

        Why does ChatGPT fail or time out on Apple Health export.zip?
        
Raw Apple Health exports contain millions of overlapping sensor rows—often 300MB to 2GB uncompressed. ChatGPT’s Python sandbox hits memory or time limits reading raw XML and frequently double-counts steps across iPhone and Apple Watch. HealthDock cleans, deduplicates, and summarizes everything locally in your browser first.

      

      

        How do I stop ChatGPT from training on my health summary?
        
Open ChatGPT → Settings → Data Controls and turn off Improve the model for everyone, or paste your summary into a Temporary Chat. HealthDock itself never transmits or stores your file anywhere.

      

      

        Does it work with free ChatGPT as well as ChatGPT Plus / Team?
        
Yes. Because context.md is compact plain Markdown (~2KB), you can paste it into free ChatGPT, Plus, Pro, or Team without hitting attachment quotas.

      

      

        Is this medical advice?
        
No. HealthDock only formats and summarizes your personal Apple Health records. Always verify health insights with a qualified clinician before making medical changes.
